    TECHNO DIVE · PRE-DIVE BRIEFING

    4 Essentials to Survive Deep Overhead

    1
    Switch Gases by Depth

    Start on EAN32 [1] down to 22m, switch to Trimix [2] for deep caves & wrecks, then back to EAN32 at 22m and O₂ 100% [3] at 6m to clear deco. Press Space (or tap the banner) for instant optimal gas.

    2
    Stay Calm & Trim Smoothly

    Finning hard near the floor stirs blinding silt, and open-circuit bubbles under fragile ceilings dislodge cascading silt (CCR rebreathers glide bubble-free). Hold Shift (or LT on gamepad) for slow frog-kicks.

    3
    Reels & Shipwreck Tools

    Press G / hold R to lay or rewind guideline reels in unlined caves. On wrecks, press E (or tap the bottom pill) to clip the shot line, crank watertight hatches, or cut free from nets. Controllers work automatically!

    4
    Reactive Hazards & Acoustic Sonar

    Watch live °C drops inside icy thermoclines, time surge-driven shifting hatches, and pulse Sonar [Q] to pierce 100% silt-outs and reveal hatches, fragile ceilings & relics. Press Esc to pause or O for Photo Studio.
